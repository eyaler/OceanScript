# Music credits

All recordings are in the public domain in the United States: they were published before 1923 (sound recordings from before 1923 entered the public domain under the Music Modernization Act) and the compositions are out of copyright. They were downloaded from the Internet Archive (Great 78 Project / community uploads), trimmed, loudness-normalised and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.
